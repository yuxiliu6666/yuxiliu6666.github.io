Long-sequence video generation is constrained by the quadratic cost of self-attention. Mixture of Distributions Matters studies dynamic sparse attention for efficient video Diffusion Transformers and adapts attention computation to the heterogeneous distributions encountered during generation.

The work was accepted by **ICML 2026**.
