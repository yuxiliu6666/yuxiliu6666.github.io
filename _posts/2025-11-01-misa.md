MISA is a memory-efficient optimization method for large language models. It uses module-wise importance sampling to reduce the memory demands of pre-training and fine-tuning while retaining the optimization signal from selected modules.

The work was accepted by **NeurIPS 2025**.

- [NeurIPS paper](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2025/poster/115158)
